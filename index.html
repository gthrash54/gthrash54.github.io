About

I am a fourth-year medical student at the UAB Heersink School of Medicine and an incoming PhD student in Neuroengineering at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. In January 2027 I join Dr. Harrison Walker's lab to work on machine-learning decoding of subthalamic nucleus recordings for adaptive deep brain stimulation and brain-computer interfaces.

My work to date spans neuromodulation for movement and sleep disorders, evidence synthesis and meta-analysis in neurosurgery and neurology, and equitable access to neurological care in low- and middle-income countries. Before medicine I studied chemistry at Birmingham-Southern College, where I also played football, and I grew up working on a potato farm in northeast Alabama.
