I am a fourth-year medical student at the UAB Heersink School of Medicine and an incoming Ph.D. student in Neuroengineering at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. In Dr. Harrison Walker’s lab I work on decoding subthalamic nucleus recordings, including evoked resonant neural activity (ERNA), for adaptive deep brain stimulation. My published work is in neuromodulation for restless legs syndrome, neurosurgical outcomes, and equitable access to neurological care.
